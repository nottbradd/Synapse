# Electromagnetic induction

A changing magnetic flux induces an electromotive force. Connect this idea to [[Physics/Magnetic flux|magnetic flux]] and [[Methods/Rates of change]].

## Faraday’s law

For a coil with $N$ turns:

$$
\mathcal{E} = -N\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt}
$$

Q: What does the negative sign in Faraday’s law represent?
A: The induced current opposes the **change** in magnetic flux that produces it (Lenz’s law).

Electromotive force :: Energy transferred per unit charge, measured in volts.
